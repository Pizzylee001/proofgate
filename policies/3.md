# ProofGate Policy #3 — "Conservative"

Lend up to 500 PGUSD to borrowers with at least 5 completed PGUSD repayments
to my vault on Ethereum Sepolia. Never exceed 500 PGUSD per release.

I am stricter than other lenders: three repayments of history is not enough
for me. Five, all proven on Sepolia via the Attestcoin Protocol, all paid in
PGUSD, all into my vault, all from the borrower's own wallet. The released
amount is the total actually repaid, capped at 500 PGUSD.
