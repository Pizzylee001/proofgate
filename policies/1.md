# ProofGate Policy #1 — "Proof-of-Repayment Microcredit"

Lend up to 50 PGUSD to any borrower with at least 3 completed repayments to my
vault on Ethereum Sepolia, paid in PGUSD. Never exceed 50 PGUSD per release.

## What this means, precisely

- The borrower must have sent me at least three separate PGUSD transfers, and
  each one must be proven to have actually happened on Ethereum Sepolia —
  not claimed, proven. The Attestcoin Protocol verifies that.
- Every repayment must land in my vault address on Sepolia. Transfers anywhere
  else do not count.
- Every repayment must be in PGUSD. A transfer of some lookalike token does
  not count.
- The borrower asking for credit must be the same wallet that made the
  repayments. Borrowed reputation does not count.
- When the AI agent approves, the released credit equals the total the
  borrower actually repaid, and never more than 50 PGUSD — no matter what
  amount the agent writes in its rationale.
