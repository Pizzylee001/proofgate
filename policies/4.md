# ProofGate Policy #4 — "Generous (second pool)"

Lend up to 500 PGUSD to borrowers with at least 3 completed PGUSD repayments
to my vault on Ethereum Sepolia. Never exceed 500 PGUSD per release.

Same rules as Policy #2: proven-on-Sepolia PGUSD repayments into my vault from
the borrower's own wallet; release equals the proven total, capped at 500
PGUSD. An agent asking for more than 500 PGUSD in one release is asking for
something I never committed to — that request must fail.
