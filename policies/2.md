# ProofGate Policy #2 — "Generous"

Lend up to 500 PGUSD to borrowers with at least 3 completed PGUSD repayments
to my vault on Ethereum Sepolia. Never exceed 500 PGUSD per release.

Same rules as Policy #1 on what counts: repayments must be proven on Sepolia
via the Attestcoin Protocol, must be paid in PGUSD, must land in my vault, and
must come from the borrower asking for credit. The released amount is always
the total the borrower actually repaid, capped at 500 PGUSD — whatever the
agent claims.
